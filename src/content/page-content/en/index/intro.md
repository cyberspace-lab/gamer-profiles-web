We strive to create typological questionnaires that are based on real data. And for that, we need you! Unfortunately, all typological questionnaires are currently in English. Many of them are also outdated and need to be adapted for today's players.

So we translate, adapt, innovate, and improve existing typologies for our Czech community. Our goal is to create functional and reliable questionnaires that will serve not only you but also future video game research!
